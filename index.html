01Research

Robot intelligence for real industrial tasks

My research focuses on robotics foundation models and dexterous manipulation, including perception, alignment, and contact-rich interaction in real industrial settings. I explore robotic algorithms and systems through autonomous insertion, dimensional measurement, and human–robot teleoperation tasks.

Research in progress

Autonomous Robotic Insertion of Industrial Connectors

This ongoing research addresses autonomous robotic insertion of industrial connectors under initial position and orientation errors. The planned approach combines visual information and contact feedback to connect target localization and alignment with local search and compliant insertion. Particular attention is given to seating verification and recovery from unsuccessful attempts, with the aim of improving reliability in practical industrial settings.

Autonomous Compressor Rotor Insertion and Shaft Diameter Measurement

This ongoing research connects autonomous robotic insertion of compressor rotors with measurement of rotor shaft diameter. It examines rotor localization, axis alignment, and insertion alongside dimensional verification. The work also explores how grasping pose, measurement location, and robot positioning repeatability affect diameter readings, aiming to establish a coherent workflow linking manipulation and measurement.

Project Experience

Teleoperation of a Dual-Arm Quadruped Robot for EOD

This project combines Marvin robotic arms, an exoskeleton, and VR for remote operation of a dual-arm quadruped robot in specialized environments. It focuses on mapping operator motion to the robot arms, coordinating arm operation with body movement, and presenting field views and robot state. These elements connect the operator interface with remote execution in a unified teleoperation workflow.

Vision- and Force-Guided Robotic Screwdriving System

This project uses a classical perception, planning, and control pipeline for robotic screwdriving. YOLO Pose keypoints and PnP geometry provide target localization and pre-alignment. Six-axis force/torque feedback informs contact detection and axial approach, coordinated with the screwdriving unit during tightening. The workflow connects target recognition, robot approach, contact-guided execution, and result verification.

Vision-Guided Robotic Suction Picking and Palletizing of Sheet Workpieces

This project combines a Marvin robotic arm, SAM3 target segmentation, and pose estimation for automated sheet picking and palletizing. The workflow links target-region extraction with suction position and orientation, transfer-path planning, and pallet-layer management. Robot motion is coordinated with suction and release timing to connect picking, transfer, and placement within a consistent handling process.

Quadruped Robot Teleoperation for Highway Operations

This project uses VR as the remote operator interface for a quadruped robot in highway operations. Operator intent is translated into locomotion and turning commands, supported by field views, body posture, and operating-state feedback. The work connects movement control, mode switching, and pause and takeover workflows, with attention to intuitive interaction and consistent operation states.
